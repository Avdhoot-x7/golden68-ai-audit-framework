📄 Published Research Paper

Complete IEEE conference manuscript generated directly from the 80-page Capstone project findings.

Golden 68 AI Audit Framework: Explainable AI for Autonomous Systems

Avadhut Makarand Chakradeo, Mayuri Mohan Sutar, Onkar Machindra Markad, Raviraj Dattatray Waghmare, and Dr. R. A. Medar
Department of Computer Science & Engineering (Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning)
K. E. Society's Rajarambapu Institute of Technology, Rajaramnagar, Maharashtra, India

Abstract: As Large Language Models are integrated into safety-critical autonomous workflows, traditional benchmarks fail to quantify statutory liability, causal resilience, and behavioral invariance. We present the Golden 68 AI Audit Framework, an explainable auditing architecture combining 68 expert-curated adversarial vectors aligned with the EU AI Act 2026. Evaluating openai/gpt-oss-120b against gemini-2.5-flash with ChromaDB statutory RAG revealed an overall score of 7.53/10, with Compliance demonstrating the highest vulnerability (6.27/10) compared to Causality (7.35/10) and Consistency (8.91/10). Inter-rater agreement with human experts achieved Cohen's Kappa κ = 0.778.
